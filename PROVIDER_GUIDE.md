## 2. Onboarding & The 6-Stage Verification Pipeline

To ensure household safety, every provider must undergo administrative verification before accepting customer bookings:

```
[1. PENDING]       -> New registration submitted.
[2. UNDER_REVIEW]  -> Documents & trade experience checked by PhiksNiyar operations.
[3. VERIFIED]      -> Activated! Eligible to receive real customer bookings.
[4. REJECTED]      -> Application denied (e.g. invalid credentials).
[5. SUSPENDED]     -> Temporarily deactivated due to policy violations or disputes.
[6. INACTIVE]      -> Off-duty or seasonal provider pause.
```

### Required Verification Documents:
1. **Government Photo ID:** Aadhaar or Voter ID (Verified securely; no plaintext Aadhaar stored on our servers).
2. **Trade Experience:** Minimum 1 year demonstrated experience in your trade (AC, Plumbing, Electrical, Appliances, etc.).
3. **Payout Account:** Bank account details or UPI ID (e.g., `phone@upi`) for weekly settlements.

## 3. Daily Job Workflow: From Dispatch to Doorstep

```
[1. Dispatch Offer] -> Review service, locality, and problem description.
[2. Tap Accept]     -> Full customer address and directions unlocked.
[3. Tap On The Way] -> Customer receives live arrival updates.
[4. Tap Arrived]    -> Customer receives a 4-digit Doorstep OTP.
[5. Enter OTP]      -> Verbal OTP entered in app to begin work.
[6. Service & Bill] -> Quote additional parts if needed (Customer must approve).
[7. Completion]     -> Collect payment via UPI or Cash confirmation.
```

### 3.1 Doorstep OTP Protocol (డోర్‌స్టెప్ OTP)
- When you arrive at the customer's house, tap **"Arrived"**.
- Ask the customer: *"దయచేసి మీ మొబైల్‌కు వచ్చిన 4 అంకెల OTP చెప్పండి"* (*"Please share the 4-digit OTP shown on your screen"*).
- Enter the OTP in your app. Once verified, the job status switches to **IN_PROGRESS**.
- **Important:** Never start work before verifying the OTP.

## 4. Quoting Replacement Parts & Additional Charges

If a job requires new spare parts (e.g., AC capacitor, washing machine belt, brass plumbing valve):
1. **Explain the issue** clearly to the customer.
2. In the PhiksNiyar app, tap **"Propose Additional Charges"**.
3. Enter the exact part cost and the reason (e.g., `₹250 - Capacitor replacement`).
4. **Ask the customer to tap "Approve" on their phone.**
5. Once approved, the booking total is automatically updated.
6. *Rule:* Never purchase or install parts without in-app customer approval.

---

## 5. Payments & Collections

PhiksNiyar supports two verified payment methods:
1. **Online UPI Payment (Recommended):**
   - The customer pays via PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, or Card directly in the app.
   - Payout is credited to your PhiksNiyar earnings instantly.
2. **Cash on Delivery (COD):**
   - Collect the exact cash amount from the customer.
   - Tap **"Report Cash Payment"** in the app.
   - The customer or PhiksNiyar admin confirms receipt to complete the job.

## 6. 7-Day Warranty & Rework Policy

Every completed job is covered by PhiksNiyar's **7-Day Customer Warranty**:
- If an issue recurs within 7 days (e.g. water leakage persists after faucet repair), the customer can request a free rework.
- You are required to visit and inspect the rework without charging inspection or labor fees.
- Reliable rework service boosts your provider rating and unlocks top priority dispatch!

## 7. Commission & Payout Schedule

- **Technician Earnings:** 90% of service charges + 100% of approved replacement parts.
- **Settlement Day:** Every **Monday by 5:00 PM** directly to your registered UPI ID or bank account.
