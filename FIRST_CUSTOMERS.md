## 2. Realistic Revenue Model

### 10% Flat Completed Commission
- Visiting charge: **₹149** (Electrician/Plumber diagnosis)
- Average repair ticket in Chilakaluripet: **₹450 – ₹1,200**
- Platform fee: **10%** of total job invoice.
- **Example:** ₹800 AC service job = **₹80 platform revenue** collected upon completion.
- Technicians keep **90%** of their labor earnings.

### Zero Upfront Registration Fees for Providers
- **No lead buying fees.** Unverified leads destroy provider trust.
- Providers pay commission **only when they get paid in cash or online** by the customer.

## 7. Customer Support & Dispute Resolution SOP

For the first 50 jobs, the founder should personally oversee booking fulfillment:
1. When a customer books, an instant SMS / WhatsApp alert is triggered.
2. Call the customer within 5 minutes to confirm their location and preferred time slot.
3. Call the nearest verified provider and dispatch the job.
4. Reminder: remind the customer to share the **4-digit Doorstep Start-OTP** only when the technician arrives at their door.
5. Once the job is completed, follow up via WhatsApp: *"Did the technician resolve your issue? Your 7-Day Warranty is active."*
